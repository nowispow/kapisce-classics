# Sense and Sensibility: Character List

## Main Characters
- **Elinor Dashwood**: The eldest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Dashwood. She represents "sense" (reason and restraint) in the novel.
- **Marianne Dashwood**: The second daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Dashwood. She represents "sensibility" (emotion and impulsiveness).
- **Edward Ferrars**: The eldest brother of Fanny Dashwood and Robert Ferrars. A kind, unassuming man who forms an attachment to Elinor.
- **John Willoughby**: A handsome, charming young man who rescues Marianne and wins her heart, but hides a darker nature.
- **Colonel Brandon**: A grave, older gentleman and friend of Sir John Middleton who falls in love with Marianne.

## Supporting Characters
- **Mrs. Dashwood**: The mother of Elinor, Marianne, and Margaret. 
- **Margaret Dashwood**: The youngest Dashwood sister.
- **John Dashwood**: The half-brother of the Dashwood sisters.
- **Fanny Dashwood**: John Dashwood's wife and Edward's sister.
- **Sir John Middleton**: A distant relation who offers the Dashwoods a cottage on his estate.
- **Mrs. Jennings**: Sir John's mother-in-law, a merry and gossipy widow.
- **Lucy Steele**: A calculating young woman who is secretly engaged to Edward Ferrars.
