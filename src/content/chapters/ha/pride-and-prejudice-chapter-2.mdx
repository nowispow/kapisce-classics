Malam Bennet na daga cikin mutanen da suka ziyarci Malam Bingley tun farko.

<Callout title="Analysis [1]" variant="explanation">
Ziyyar da Malam Bennet ya kai a boye ya nuna cewa yana cikin ƙoƙarin taimakawa 'ya'yansa game da makomarsu. Wannan irin magana yana aiki a matsayin kariya daga yadda matarsa ke ƙoƙarin yin abubuwa a cikin al'umma. Halayyensa ya nuna cewa yana kula da 'ya'yansa, amma yana ɓoye wannan a bayyanar da ba ya son su.
</Callout>
Yana son ziyarce shi, amma har zuwa ƙarshe yana tabbatar wa matarsa cewa kada ya je; har sai da ya ziyarce shi, matarsa ba ta san ba. Daga nan ne aka bayyana hakan a hanya mai zuwa.
[Hoto: Wani zane na Renaissance na Elizabeth Bennet a cikin ɗaki mai haske, tana yin gyare-gyare a kan hula.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-1b.png)
Da ya ga ɗayar 'yarsa tana yin gyare-gyare a kan hula, sai ya ce mata:

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">I na fata Malam Bingley zai so wannan, Lizzy.</Callout>

"Ba mu da hanyar sanin abin da Malam Bingley yake so ba," in ji mahaifiyarta, da takaici, "domin ba za mu ziyarce shi ba."

"Amma kin manta, mama," in ji Elizabeth, "za mu sake haduwa da shi a wajen taron, kuma Mrs. Long ta yi alkawari za ta gabatar da shi."

"Ban yarda Mrs. Long za ta yi haka ba. Tana da 'ya'ya mata biyu. Ita mace ce mai son kai, kuma ba na da wata ra'ayi a kanta."

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">
Yadda Mrs. Bennet ta yi watsi da Mrs. Long ya nuna yadda ake ƙoƙarin samun abokan aure. An yi la'akari da waɗanda ke kusa da su a matsayin masu buƙatar neman abokan aure a wannan yanayin. Wannan ya nuna yadda ake neman abubuwa a cikin al'umma a lokacin Regency na Ingila.
</Callout>

"Ni ma," in ji Malam Bennet; "kuma ina farin ciki da na ga ba ki dogara a kan ita ba."

Mrs. Bennet ba ta yi wani amsa ba; amma, saboda ba ta iya jure wa, sai ta fara zagi daya daga cikin 'ya'yanta.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="important">"Kada ki ci gaba da tari, Kitty, saboda Allah! Ki nuna ƙasƙanci. Kin naɗe zuciyata. Kin naɗe ta."</Callout>

[Hoto: Wani zane na Renaissance na Mrs. Bennet da ke cikin damuwa, tana riƙe da gyallen.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-2b.png)

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">Kitty ba ta da hankali a yayin tari; tana yin haka a lokacin da ba ya da kyau.</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">
Tarin Kitty yana aiki a matsayin alamar damuwar da take ciki. Malam Bennet yana daukar wannan halayyar da ke faruwa a gida a matsayin wani abu da ake nuna wa jama'a. Yana amfani da rashin lafiyarta a matsayin hanyar yin dariya a kan yadda matarsa ke damuwa.
</Callout>

[Hoto: Wani zane na Renaissance na Kitty Bennet da ke tari a cikin gyallen, yayin da mahaifinta ke kallon ta.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-3b.png)

"Ban yi tari domin jin daɗi ba," in ji Kitty, da takaici. "Sannan lokacin da za a yi wani biki, Lizzy?"

"Gobe."

"E, haka ne," in ji mahaifiyarta, "kuma Mrs. Long ba za ta dawo ba har sai gobe; don haka, ba za ta iya gabatar da shi ba, domin ita ma ba ta san shi ba."

“[To, abokina, za a iya samun amfani ne daga abokanka, kuma ka gabatar da
Mista Bingley ga _ita_.]”

“[Ba zai yiwu ba, Mista Bennet, ba zai yiwu ba, idan ban san shi ba; ta yaya za ka iya yin irin wannan wasa?]”

“[Ina godiya da hankalinka.]”

<Callout title="Analysis [4]" variant="explanation">
Yabon da Mista Bennet ya yi wa matarsa game da 'hankalinta' ya nuna tsarin zamantakewar da ake bi a wancan lokacin. Ya nuna cewa 'sanin' al'umma yawanci yana da zurfi kuma ana yin shi don nuna wani abu. Wannan abin da ya faru yana nuna cewar tsarin da makwabtansu ke da shi ba shi da amfani.
</Callout>
Tabbas, sanin juna na tsawon makonni biyu ba ya da yawa. Ba za a iya sanin abin da wani mutum yake ba a ƙarshen makonni biyu. Amma idan _mu_ ba za mu yi ƙoƙari ba, wani zai yi; kuma bayan duka, Mrs. Long da 'ya'yanta za su ci gaba; saboda haka, idan ta yi tunanin cewa hakan zai zama alheri, idan ka ki aikin, zan yi shi.]”

'Yan mata sun kalli ubansu. Mrs. Bennet ta ce kawai, "[Abin ban dariya, abin ban dariya!]"

“[Menene ma'anar wannan magana mai karfi?]” ya ce. “[Shin kana ganin yadda ake gabatar da mutane, da kuma mahimmancin da ake ba wa hakan, a matsayin abin ban dariya? Ba zan yarda da hakan ba. Me ce, Mary? Domin ina san ka, matashi, kuma kana son tunani, kana karanta manyan littattafai, kuma kana buga fassarori.]”

[Hoton wani zane na Renaissance na Mary Bennet, wanda manyan littattafai suka kewaye, kuma tana jin tsoro.]

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">
An gano Mary ne ta hanyar sha'awar sa na samun daraja a fannin ilimi, amma ba ta da 'hira' kamar Elizabeth. Rashin iya samun wani abu 'mai amfani' da zai faɗa yana nuna rawar da take takawa a matsayin wani abu mai ban dariya a cikin gidan. Ta zama alama da take nuna cewar ba ya dace a nuna hikima ba tare da sanin abubuwa ba.
</Callout>

Mary ta so ta faɗa wani abu mai amfani, amma ba ta san yadda za ta yi ba.

“[Yayin da Mary take gyara ra'ayoyinta,]” ya ci gaba, “[bari mu koma ga Mista Bingley.]”

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="summary">Ina da damuwa game da Mista Bingley.</Callout>

“[Na yi takaici da jin hakan; amma me yasa ba ka faɗa mini a da? Idan na san hakan a wannan lokacin, tabbas ba zan ziyarce shi ba. Hakan ba abu ne mai kyau ba; amma…”

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="important">
Yanzu da na ziyarci, ba za mu iya guje wa wannan haduwa ba.
</Callout>

[Hoton wani babban zane na Renaissance na dangin Bennet, inda suka taru yayin da Mista Bennet yake bayyana ziyarar da ya yi.]

Abin da 'yan mata suka ji shi ne abin da ya so—abin da Mrs. Bennet ta ji, wanda ya iya fi sauran. Duk da haka, bayan da jin daɗin ya ƙare, ta fara bayyana cewa hakan ne abin da ta yi tsammani tun da.

<Callout title="Analysis [6]" variant="explanation">
Canjin da Mrs. Bennet ta yi, daga rashin jin daɗi zuwa jin daɗi, yana nuna cewa tana da sauƙin canji kuma ba ta da zurfi. Da zarar ta yi jayayya cewa "ta yi tsammani" labarin, hakan ya zama hanyar da take amfani da ita don kare alfahuminta. Wannan sauƙin canji shi ne babban batu a cikin yadda Austen ta bayyana halayyar Mrs. Bennet.
</Callout>

"Yaya kake da kyau, abokina, Mista Bennet! Amma na san cewa a ƙarshe zan iya sa ka yarda. Na tabbata cewa ka fi son yarankanka, don haka ba za ka iya barin kuɗin ba. To, yaya nake farin ciki! Kuma wannan abin dariya ne, cewa ka tafi a wannan safe, kuma ba ka faɗa komai game da hakan har yanzu."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Yanzu, Kitty, za ki iya yin tari da yawa kamar yadda kike so.</Callout>
Kuma, yayin da yake faɗa, ya bar ɗakin, yana gajiya da farin cikinsa.

![Wani zane na Renaissance na Mista Bennet yana barin ɗakin, yana da rauni saboda farin cikin matarsa.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-6b.png)

"Yaya kyawawan uba kuke da shi, yarinya," ta ce, lokacin da an rufe ƙofar. "Ban san yadda za ku iya ba shi godiya ba; ko ni ma, don wannan. A rayuwarmu, ba abu ne mai daɗi ba, in gaya muku, don yin sabbin abokai a kowace rana; amma don ku, za mu yi duk abin da za mu iya."
<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">
Lydia, soyayya na, duk da cewa ku ne ƙarami, ina da tabbacin cewa Mista Bingley zai rawa da ku a wajen rawa na gaba.
</Callout>

"Oh," Lydia ta ce, da ƙarfi, "Ban tsorata ba; duk da cewa ni ne ƙarami, ni ne na dogo."

<Callout title="Analysis [7]" variant="explanation">
Yarinyar Lydia ta yi iƙirarin game da tsawonta, wanda ya nuna halinta na rashin hankali da rashin tsoro. Ta ga duniya a matsayin wani abu mai ƙarfi, wanda ba ya da hankali ko kuma taka-tsantsan kamar Elizabeth. Wannan tattaunawa na farko ya nuna rashin hankalin matashi wanda zai jawo ta a cikin labarin.
</Callout>

![Wani zane na Renaissance na Lydia Bennet, wacce ke tsaye da ƙarfi da kuma da ƙarfi](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-7a.png)
Sauran dare an yi ta tunanin yadda Mista Bennet zai dawo, da kuma lokacin da za su tambaye shi a kan abinci.
