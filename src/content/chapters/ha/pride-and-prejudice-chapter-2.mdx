Malam Bennet yana cikin waɗanda suka jiran zuwan Malam Bingley.

<Callout title="Analysis [1]" variant="explanation">

Ziyarar da Malam Bennet ya yi a boye ta nuna cewa yana da himma a rayuwar 'ya'yansa. Wannan irin maganar da yake yi yana aiki a matsayin kariya ga matarsa da ke kokarin yin abubuwa da yawa a al'umma. Halayensa ya nuna cewa yana kula da 'ya'yansa, amma yana boye wannan ainihin.

</Callout>

Yana da niyyar zuwa ziyarce shi, amma a karshe, ya ci gaba da tabbatar wa matarsa cewa ba zai tafi ba; har zuwa maraice bayan ziyarar, ba ta san ba. Daga nan sai aka bayyana a hanya mai zuwa.

[Hoto: Hoto na zane na lokacin Renaissance na Elizabeth Bennet a ɗaki mai haske, tana aiki akan wani hula.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-1b.png)
Da yake kallon ɗiyarsa ta biyu tana aiki a kan wani hula, sai ya ce mata da sauri,

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

I na fatan Malam Bingley zai so shi, Lizzy.

</Callout>

"Ba mu da hanyar sanin abin da Malam Bingley yake so," in ji mahaifiyarta, cikin takaici, "tun da ba za mu ziyarci shi ba."

"Amma kin manta, mama," in ji Elizabeth, "za mu hadu da shi a wajen taruwar, kuma Mrs. Long ta yi alkawarin gabatar da shi."

"Ban yi imani Mrs. Long za ta yi komai. Tana da 'ya'ya mata guda biyu. Ita mace ce mai son kai, kuma ban da wata ra'ayin game da ita."

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">

Kokin da Mrs. Bennet ta yi wa Mrs. Long ya nuna yadda ake kishiya a kasuwar auren. A wannan yanayi, ana kallon makwabta a matsayin masu gasa a kan samun maza da ake so. Wannan yana nuna yadda al'umma ke aiki a lokacin zamanin Regency na Ingila.

</Callout>

"Ni ma, babu," in ji Malam Bennet; "kuma ina farin ciki da na ga ba ki dogara a kan ta ba."

Mrs. Bennet ba ta yi wani amsa ba; amma, saboda ba ta iya jurewa, sai ta fara zagin ɗayansa.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="important">

"Kar ki ci gaba da tari, Kitty, saboda Allah! Ki ji tausayi a kan jikin nan. Kina lalata shi."

</Callout>

[Hoto: Hoto na lokacin Renaissance na Mrs. Bennet a yanayi mai damuwa, tana rike da wani tsuntsun.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-2b.png)

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">

Kitty ba ta da hankali a kan tari, tana yin tari a lokacin da ba ya kamata.

</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">

Tarin Kitty yana aiki a matsayin alama ne na matsalolin da ke faruwa a gida. Malam Bennet yana kallon wannan halayyar da ke faruwa a cikin gidan a matsayin wani irin wasan kwaikwayo. Yana amfani da rashin lafiyarta a matsayin hanya don yin dariya a kan matarsa.

</Callout>

[Hoto: Hoto na lokacin Renaissance na Kitty Bennet tana tari a cikin tsuntsun, yayin da mahaifinta ke kalle ta.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-3b.png)

"Ban yi tari domin jin daɗi ba," in ji Kitty, cikin damuwa. "A wane lokaci za a yi wani taron, Lizzy?"

"A gobe."

"Ee, haka ne," in ji mahaifiyarta, "kuma Mrs. Long ba za ta dawo ba har sai ranar da ta gabata; don haka, ba zai yuwu a gare ta ta gabatar da shi, saboda ba za ta san shi ba."

"Sai, abokaciyata, za ka iya samun amfani daga abokinka, kuma ka gabatar da
Mista Bingley ga _ita_."

"Ba zai yiwu ba, Mista Bennet, ba zai yiwu ba, idan ban san shi ba; ta yaya za ka iya yin wannan? "

"Ina girmama hankalinkai."

<Callout title="Analysis [4]" variant="explanation">

Yabon Mista Bennet ga 'hankalin' matarsa ya nuna tsarin al'ada na lokacin. Ya nuna cewa "sanin al'ada" yawanci yana da wuya kuma ana yin shi ne don nuna wani abu. Wannan ba daidai ba yana nuna rashin ma'anar abin da makwabtansu ke so.

</Callout>

Wani lokaci na makwanni biyu tabbas ba ya isa. Ba za ka iya sanin wani mutum ba a lokacin. Amma idan _mu_ ba za mu yi ƙoƙari ba, wani zai yi; kuma bayan duka, Mrs. Long da 'ya'yanta za su yi ƙoƙari; kuma, don haka, idan za ta yi tunanin cewa kana nuna kyauta, idan ka ƙi, zan yi shi da kaina.

'Ya'yan sun kalli ubansu. Mrs. Bennet ta ce kawai, "Ba komai, ba komai!"

"Menene ma'anar wannan magana?" ya ce. "Shin kana ganin yadda ake gabatarwa, da mahimmancin da ake ba wa hakan, a matsayin wani abu mara ma'ana? Ba zan yarda da hakan ba. Ki ce, Mary? Domin ni na san cewa kai budurwa ce mai tunani, kuma ke karanta manyan littattafai, kuma ke fitar da abubuwa."

![Wani zane na lokacin Renaissance na Mary Bennet, wanda manyan littattafai suka kewaye shi, kuma tana jin tsoro.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-4b.png)

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">

Mary ta nuna sha'awar samun matsayi a ilimi, amma ba ta da "hankali" kamar Elizabeth. Rashin ikon ta na samun wani abu 'mai ma'ana' da za ta ce yana nuna rawar da take takawa a matsayin wani ɓangaren nishadi a cikin iyali. Tana aiki a matsayin gargaɗi game da yin ƙoƙari a kan hikima ba tare da gaskiya ba.

</Callout>

Mary ta so ta ce wani abu mai ma'ana, amma ba ta san yadda za ta yi ba.

"Yayin da Mary take gyara ra'ayinta," ya ci gaba, "mu koma ga Mista Bingley."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="summary">

Ina da ƙiyayya ga Mista Bingley.

</Callout>

"Ina ba ka haƙuri da jin hakan; amma me yasa ba ka faɗa mini ba tun da? Idan na san hakan a yau, tabbas ba zan je wurinsa ba. Abu ne mai ba da mamaki; amma..."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="important">

Yanzu da na je wurinsa, ba za mu iya guje wa wannan ba.

</Callout>

![Wani babban yanayi na lokacin Renaissance na iyalin Bennet, inda Mista Bennet yake bayyana ziyarar da ya yi a asirce.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-5b.png)
Sha'awar 'yan mata tabbas shine abin da ya so - wanda Mrs. Bennet ya fi duka; amma bayan da farin cikin da ya fara ya ƙare, sai ta fara bayyana cewa shi ne abin da ta yi tsammani.

<Callout title="Analysis [6]" variant="explanation">

Canjin yanayin Mrs. Bennet zuwa ga farin ciki ya nuna cewa tana da yanayi mai sauyawa da kuma sha'awa. Batun da ta ce cewa 'ta yi tsammani' yana nuna cewa tana ƙoƙarin kare alfaramanta. Wannan yanayin yana da mahimmanci a cikin yadda Austen ta nuna halayyar ta.

</Callout>

"Yana da kyau a gare ka, mai girma Mr. Bennet! Amma na san cewa a ƙarshe zan iya sa ka yarda. Na tabbata cewa kana son 'ya'yanku sosai, don haka ba za ka bar wani aboki ba. To, ina farin ciki! Kuma wannan abu ne mai ban sha'awa, cewa ka tafi a wannan safe, kuma ba ka faɗa komai game da shi har yanzu."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

Yanzu, Kitty, za ki iya yin taƙunya kamar yadda kike so.

</Callout>

Kuma, yayin da yake faɗa, ya bar ɗakin, yana jin gajiya da farin cikin matarsa.

![Wani zane na Renaissance na Mr. Bennet yana barin ɗakin, yana da ƙarfin hali saboda farin cikin matarsa.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-6b.png)

"Ya ɗaya, mahaifinku mai kyau, 'ya'ya," in ji ta, lokacin da an rufe kofa. "Ban san yadda za ku iya ba shi godiya ba saboda kyawunsa; ko ni ma. A zamani namu, ba abu ne mai daɗi ba, kamar yadda zan faɗa muku, a yi sabbin abokai kowace rana; amma saboda ku, za mu yi duk abin da za mu iya."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">

Lydia, soyayyata, duk da cewa ita ce ƙarama, ina da tabbacin cewa Mr. Bingley zai rawa da ku a lokacin wata babbar taron.

</Callout>

"Oh," in ji Lydia, da ƙarfi, "Ban ji tsoro ba; saboda duk da cewa ita ce ƙarama, ita ce mafi tsayi."

<Callout title="Analysis [7]" variant="explanation">

Yin gogi na Lydia game da tsawonta yana nuna ta kasancewa mai sauri da rashin hankali. Tana kallon al'umma da ƙarfin hali, wanda ba shi da wani abu kamar Elizabeth. Wannan tattaunawa na farko yana bayyana wani abu mai ban sha'awa wanda zai ƙara ƙarfafawa a lokacin.

</Callout>

![Wani zane na Renaissance na Lydia Bennet, mai ƙarama, tana tsaye da ƙarfin hali.](../metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-2/scene-7a.png)

Sauran lokacin da ya rage an yi amfani da shi wajen tunanin yadda zai dawo da ziyarar Mr. Bennet, kuma an yanke shawarar lokacin da za su yi masa tambaya game da ci.
