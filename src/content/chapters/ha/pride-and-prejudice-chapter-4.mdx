"Oh, kina da sauƙin son mutane. Ba a taba ganin wani a cikin ku ba. Duk duniya suna da kyau kuma suna da kyau a idanun ki. Ban taɓa jin ki tana magana a kan wani ba a rayuwata."

"Ina so in guji yin hukunci da wani; amma koyaushe ina fada abin da nake tunani."

Don haka, kina son 'yan uwansa, ko? Halayyensu ba su daidaita da nashi."

"Tabbas ba a farko; amma suna da kyau idan kina yin magana da su. Miss Bingley za ta zauna tare da ɗan'uwanta, kuma za ta kula da gidansa; kuma ina da tabbaci cewa za mu sami wata kyakkyawar makwabtatuwa a cikin ta."

Elizabeth ta saurara cikin shiru, amma ba ta yarda ba: halayyensu a wajen taron ba a nufin su zama abin farin ciki; kuma tare da saurin lura da kuma rashin haƙuri fiye da 'yar uwanta, kuma
tare da hukunci, kuma ba a yi wani ƙoƙari don yin magana a kan ta ba, ba ta son amince da su.

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">
Elizabeth tana aiki a matsayin wanda ke nuna ra'ayi mai amfani a kan ra'ayin Jane, tana amfani da saurin tunaninta don bincika halayyar al'umma. Ta gane girman kai da son kai na 'yan uwanta Bingley, yayin da Jane kawai ta ga kyawawan halayyar su. Hukuncinta ya ƙara ƙarfi saboda rashin sha'awar ta a kan amincewar 'yan uwanta Bingley.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">
Sun kasance, a asali, mata masu kyau; ba su da wani matsala a cikin kyawawan halayyar su idan sun ji daɗi, kuma ba su da matsala a cikin son yin abin da suke so; amma sun kasance masu girman kai da son kai.
</Callout>

Sun kasance masu kyau; an koyar da su a ɗaya daga cikin manyan makarantun da ke garin; suna da dukiyar dubu dari; suna da al'adar kashe kuɗi fiye da yadda ya kamata, kuma suna yin hulɗa da mutane masu daraja; saboda haka, a kowane fanni, suna da hakkin yin tunanin kyau game da kansu kuma suna yin tunanin ba daidai game da wasu.

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">
Asalin dukiyar Bingley a kasuwanci wani bayani ne mai mahimmanci wanda ke shafar matsayinsu a al'umma da damuwarsu. 'Yan uwanta sun fi damuwa da hulɗarsu da mutane masu daraja fiye da tarihin nasarar danginsu. Wannan yanayin yana nuna bambance-bambancen zamantakewar Regency na Ingila.
</Callout>

Mista Bingley ya gaji dukiyar da ta kai kusan dubu dari daga mahaifinsa, wanda ya yi niyyar sayen wani gida, amma bai rayu ya yi hakan ba.

<Callout title="Analysis [4]" variant="explanation">
Yanke shawarar Mista Bingley na yin hayar Netherfield House maimakon sayen gida yana nuna 'sauƙin' halayyar sa. Ya gamsu da halin da yake ciki kuma bai ji wani buƙatar yin wani abu na dindindin ba. Wannan halayyar sa ta sa ya zama aboki mai kyau ga Darcy mai yanke shawara da mai buƙata.
</Callout>

Mista Bingley ya yi niyyar yin hakan, kuma a wasu lokuta ya zaɓi gundumar sa; amma, yanzu da ya samu gida mai kyau da kuma 'yancin wani gida, ya zama wani abu da ake tambaya ga waɗanda suka san sauƙin halayyar sa, ko ba zai iya yin hakan ba. ya yi sauran rayuwarsa a Netherfield, kuma ya bar ƙarni na gaba ya sayi.

'Yan uwansa sun damu sosai game da shi don ya samu gida na kanshi; amma, duk da cewa yanzu ya zama kawai mai hayar, Miss Bingley ba ta yarda da yin shiga a teburinsa; kuma Mrs. Hurst, wacce ta auri wani mutum mai kyau fiye da dukiyarsa, ba ta son yin tunani.
yana amfani da gidansa a matsayin gidanta idan ya dace da ita. Mr. Bingley bai kai shekaru biyu ba lokacin da aka jarraba shi, ta hanyar wata shawara da aka yi, ya ziyarci gidan Netherfield. Ya ziyarce shi, kuma ya duba shi, na rabin sa'a; ya ji daɗin yanayin da manyan ɗakuna, ya yarda da abin da mai shi ya faɗa game da shi, kuma ya ɗauke shi nan take.

A tsakanin shi da Darcy, akwai wata kusanci mai ƙarfi, duk da bambancin halayensu.

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">
Kusancin da ke tsakanin Darcy da Bingley an gina shi ne akan wata kusanci mai ƙarfi, duk da bambancin halayensu. Darcy yana girmama buɗewar Bingley, yayin da Bingley ya dogara sosai kan sanin Darcy da kuma yadda yake yin hukunci. Haɗin kansu ya nuna cewa, a wasu lokuta, iya fahimtar juna na iya shawo kan bambance-bambancen halaye.
</Callout>

Bingley ya ƙaunace Darcy saboda sauƙin, buɗewar, da kuma yadda yake daidaita halayensa, duk da cewa babu wani halaye da za su bambanta da nasa, kuma duk da cewa ba ya jin rashin jin daɗi. Dangane da kusancin Darcy, Bingley yana da dogon aminci, kuma yana da mafi kyawun ra'ayi game da yadda yake yin hukunci.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

<Callout title="Analysis [6]" variant="explanation">

Bingley yana ganin kowa yana da kyau kuma yana da tabbacin cewa za a ƙaunace shi, yayin da Darcy yana ganin kowa yana da nakasa kuma yana haifar da rashin jin daɗi. Wannan bambanci ya ƙaddara yadda za su yi a cikin al'ummar Meryton.

Yadda suka yi magana game da taron Meryton ya nuna halayensu. Bingley bai taɓa ganin mutane masu kyau ko 'yan mata masu kyau ba a rayuwarsa; kowa ya nuna masa kyau da kulawa; babu wani tsari, babu wani tsauri; ya ji kamar ya san kowa a ɗakin; kuma game da Miss Bennet, ba zai iya tunanin wani mala'ika mai kyau ba. Darcy, a gefe ɗaya, ya ga tarin mutane da ba su da kyau kuma ba su da kyau, kuma ba ya ji wani sha'awa a gare su, kuma ba ya karɓi kulawa ko jin daɗi daga kowa daga cikinsu.
