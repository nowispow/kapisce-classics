“Eh; amma ya yi kama da cewa ya fi son na biyu.”

“Oh, ina nufin Jane, in ji, domin ya yi rawa da ita sau biyu. Tabbas, ya yi kama da cewa ya ji dadin ta – a gaskiya, ina da tabbacin cewa ya yi – na ji wani abu game da hakan – amma ba na da tabbas abin da – wani abu game da Mr. Robinson.”

“Wataƙila, ina nufin abin da na ji a tsakanin su da Mr. Robinson: ba na faɗa maka ba? Mr. Robinson ya tambaye shi yadda yake jin dadin taron Meryton, kuma ko ya yi tunanin cewa akwai mata masu kyau da yawa a ɗakinka, kuma _wa_ ya fi shi kyau? Kuma ya amsa nan take ga wannan tambayar,

Nan take kuma Mr. Bingley ya bayyana kyawun Jane a gaban Mr. Robinson, wannan ya ƙara ƙarfafa matsayinta a matsayin 'yarinya mai kyau' a cikin al'umma. Wannan sha'awar da ba a iya misaltawa ita ce abin da Mrs. Bennet take so ga ci gaban iyalanta a al'umma. Wannan ya samar da tushe na fata na soyayya wanda zai ci gaba da labarin a cikin wasu shafuka masu zuwa.

“_Abin da na ji_ ya fi amfani fiye da _abin da ki ji_, Eliza,” in ji Charlotte. “Mr. Darcy ba shi da kyau a saurara masa kamar abokinsa, shi ne?”

“In roƙe ki kada ki sa a zuciyar Lizzy ta yi baƙin ciki saboda yadda yake mu'amala da ita, domin shi mutum ne mai ban tausayi, don haka zai zama mummunan abu a ji dadinsa. Mrs. Long ta faɗa mani jiya, cewa ya zauna kusa da ita na rabin awa ba tare da ya buɗe bakinsa sau ɗaya ba.”

“Kina da tabbacin, madam? Akwai wani ɓaƙo?” in ji Jane. “Tabbas, na ga Mr. Darcy yana magana da ita.”

“Eh, domin a ƙarshe ta tambaye shi yadda yake jin Netherfield, kuma ba zai iya guje wa amsa mata ba; amma ta ce ya yi fushi da yadda ake magana da shi.”

“Miss Bingley ta faɗa mani,” in ji Jane, “kuma ba ya magana da yawa sai dai idan yana tare da abokansa. Tare da _su_, yana da kyau sosai.”

“Ba na gaskata da wani abu daga cikin wannan, ƙaunataccen. Idan ya kasance mai kyau sosai, da ya yi magana da Mrs. Long. “Ba na son ya daina magana da Mrs. Long,” in ji Miss Lucas, “amma ina so ya yi rawa da Eliza.”

“Wani lokaci, Lizzy,” in ce mahaifiyarta, “ba zan yi rawa da shi ba, idan ni ne a matsayinka.”

“Girman kai,” in ce Miss Lucas, “ba ya damun ni kamar yadda girman kai yake damun wasu, saboda akwai dalili a cikinsa. Ba za a iya mamakin cewa matashi mai kyau, mai iyali, mai arziki, da duk abin da yake so, ya yi girman kai.”

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">
Idan zan iya fada, yana da hakkin yin girman kai.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">
Tunanin Charlotte na cewa Darcy yana da “hakkin yin girman kai” saboda iyali da arziki, yana nuna wani tunani na zamani. Ta ba da shawarar cewa matsayi mai girma a al'umma yana nuna girman kai. Wannan tunani yana sabawa ra'ayin Elizabeth na cewa girman kai yana da illa, duk da matsayin da mutum yake da shi a al'umma.
</Callout>

“Wannan gaskiya ce,” in ce Elizabeth.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

<Callout title="Analysis [6]" variant="explanation">
Tunanin Elizabeth na cewa za ta iya gafarta wa Darcy girman kansa, idan ba ya sa ta jin kunya, wani muhimmin lokaci ne na tunanin kanta. Yana nuna cewa ra'ayinta game da shi ya samo asali ne daga abin da ta ji, ba daga binciken halinsa ba. Wannan rauni yana sa halinta ta fi dacewa, kuma yana bayyana raunin halinta.
</Callout>

“Girman kai,” in ce Mary, wacce ta yi alfahari da tunaninta, “yana da illa, ina da imani. Duka abin da na karanta, na yi imani cewa yana da illa; halin ɗan Adam yana da sauƙin yin girman kai, kuma akwai ƴan mu kaɗan da ba sa son yin girman kai game da wani abu, na gaskiya ko na tunanin su. Girman kai da girman kai daban ne, duk da cewa ana amfani da kalmomin biyun a wata hanya. Mutum na iya yin girman kai ba tare da ya yi girman kai ba.”

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">
Girman kai yana da alaƙa da ra'ayinmu game da kanmu; girman kai yana da alaƙa da abin da muke so wasu su yi tunani game da mu.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [7]" variant="explanation">
Tunanin Mary Bennet game da girman kai da girman kai, yana aiki a matsayin tushen labarin. Ta bayyana bambanci tsakanin ra'ayin mutum game da kansa (girman kai) da abin da mutum yake so wasu su yi tunani (girman kai). Tunaninta, duk da cewa yana da wani ɗan ɗan, yana ba da wani tsari mai mahimmanci don binciken ayyukan mutane.
</Callout>
