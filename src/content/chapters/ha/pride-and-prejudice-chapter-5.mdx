Sir William Lucas, a da, yana kasuwanci a Meryton, inda ya sami kuɗi mai yawa, kuma ya sami daraja ta zama knight ta hanyar bayar da jawabi ga sarki yayin lokacin da yake shugaban birni. <Callout title="Analysis [1]" variant="explanation">
Canjin da Sir William Lucas ya yi daga kasuwa zuwa zama knight, yana nuna yadda darajar mutum ke canzawa a Meryton. Samun wannan daraja ya sa ya ƙi aikin da yake yi a da, kuma ya sami sabon fahimtar darajarsa. Duk da wannan daraja, ya ci gaba da zama mai kyau da abokantaka, wanda ya bambanta da sanyi na Darcy.
</Callout>

Saboda, duk da cewa darajarsa ta sa shi jin daɗi, ba ta sa shi zama mai girman kai; a maimakon haka, ya kula da kowa. Ta yanayin halin sa, yana da kyau, mai abokantaka, kuma mai taimako, bayyanar sa a St. James's ya sa shi zama mai kyau.

Lady Lucas wata mata ce mai kyau, ba ta da hankali sosai don ta zama makwabtakiya mai amfani ga Mrs. Bennet. Sun haifi yara da yawa. Mafi tsufa daga cikin su, wata matashiya mai hankali da basira, mai shekaru 27, ita ce abokiyar Elizabeth.

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">
Charlotte Lucas an gabatar da ita a matsayin abokiyar Elizabeth, kuma wata matashiya mai hankali da basira, mai shekaru 27. Ɗa'a ta sa ta iya duba girman kai na Darcy da gaskiya fiye da yadda dangin Bennets za su iya yi. Ita ce wata alama ta ra'ayin zamantakewa, wanda ya bambanta da ra'ayoyin Elizabeth.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">
An bayyana taron da aka yi tsakanin dangin Bennets da Lucases don tattauna game da taron a matsayin wani "abu mai mahimmanci" wanda dole ne ya faru. Wannan al'ada ta ba dangin damar kwatanta "abubuwan da suka ji" kuma ta ƙarfafa darajarsu a matsayin wani rukuni. Ta nuna yadda al'umma ke sarrafa kuma ta samar da ra'ayoyin zamantakewa ta hanyar yin magana da kuma yin hukunci.
</Callout>

kuma a safiyar bayan taron.
"An kawo ta ta baya zuwa Longbourn don ta ji kuma ta yi magana.

'Ki fara wannan dare da kyau, Charlotte,' in ji Mrs. Bennet, da murmushi. 'Kai ne abin da Mr. Bingley ya fi so.'

'Eh; amma ya yi kama kamar ya fi son wacce ta biyo baya.'

'Oh, ina nufin Jane, in dai haka, saboda ya yi raye-raye da ita sau biyu. Tabbas, yana nuna kamar yana son ta – da gaske, ina tunanin haka – na ji wani abu game da shi – amma ba na da tabbas game da abin da ya shafi Mr. Robinson.'

'Wataƙila, ina nufin abin da na ji a tsakanin su Mr. Robinson: ba na fa ce maka ba? Mr. Robinson ya tambaye shi yadda yake jin taron da suke yi a Meryton, kuma ko ya yi tunanin cewa akwai mata masu kyau da yawa a ɗakin, kuma wacce ya fi so? Ya amsa nan take da wannan tambaya.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Oh, babbar 'yar Mrs. Bennet, babu shakka: ba za a iya samun ra'ayoyi daban-daban game da wannan.</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [4]" variant="explanation">
Mr. Bingley ya bayyana nan take kuma a bainar jama'a yadda Jane yake so, wannan ya ƙara ƙarfafa matsayinta a matsayin 'yar budurwa' ta gari. Wannan aiki na gaske shi ne abin da Mrs. Bennet take so ga ci gaban 'ya'yanta a al'umma. Yana haifar da wani yanayi na bege na soyayya wanda zai ƙarfafa labarin a cikin wasu daruruwan labarai.
</Callout>

![Wani zane na Renaissance da aka yi da furen, inda aka nuna Mr. Bingley yana dariya a lokacin taron.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-5/scene-4b.png)

'Ina alkawarin! To, hakan ya tabbata – da gaske, yana nuna kamar – amma, duk da haka, zai iya zama babu komai, kamar yadda kike sani.'

'Abin da na ji ya fi na naka amfani, Eliza,' in ji Charlotte. 'Mr. Darcy ba ya da kyau a saurara masa kamar abokinsa, shin?'
<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">
Tsohuwar Eliza! Don kawai ta zama wacce ake iya jurewa.
</Callout>

'Ina roƙonka kada ka sa Lizzy ta yi fushi saboda yadda yake yi mata, saboda shi mutum ne mai ban dariya, don haka zai zama wani abu marar kyau a samu sha'awar shi. Mrs. Long ta ce mani jiya, ya zauna kusa da ita na tsawon minti 30 ba tare da ya buɗe bakinsa ba.'

'Kina da tabbas, Madam? Ba a yi wani kuskure ba?' in ji Jane.
'Tabbas, na ga Mr. Darcy yana magana da ita.'

'Eh, saboda a ƙarshe ta tambaye shi yadda yake ji game da Netherfield, kuma ba zai iya guje wa amsa mata ba; amma ta ce ya yi fushi sosai da yadda ake magana da shi.'

'Miss Bingley ta ce mani,' in ji Jane, 'ba ya magana da yawa sai dai idan yana tare da abokan da yake kusa da su. Tare da su, yana da kyau sosai.'

'Ba na yarda da abin da kike faɗa, abokiyata. Idan ya kasance mai kyau sosai, da ya yi magana da Mrs. Long. Amma zan iya hasasawa yadda abin ya faru; kowa yana cewa yana da girma, kuma na yi imani ya ji wani abu cewa Mrs. Long ba ta da keke, kuma ya zo taron ne da wani keke mai sauƙi.'

“Ba na son ya daina magana da Mrs. Long,” in ji Miss Lucas, “amma ina so ya yi rawa da Eliza.”

“Wani lokaci, Lizzy,” in ce mahaifiyarta, “ba zan yi rawa da shi ba, idan ni ce ke.”

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Ina da imani, madam, zan iya alkawarta cewa ba zan yi rawa da shi ba.</Callout>

“Girma da ya yi,” in ce Miss Lucas, “ba ta damu da ni kamar yadda girma ke damuwa, domin akwai dalili a cikinta. Ba za a iya mamaki cewa matashi mai kyau, mai iyali, mai dukiya, kuma komai yana tare da shi, zai yi tunanin kansa.”
<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Idan zan iya fadar hakan, yana da hakkin yin girma.</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">
Dalilin Charlotte na cewa Darcy yana da 'hakkin yin girma' saboda iyali da dukiyarsa, ya kawo wani ra'ayi na gaba daya. Ta ba da shawarar cewa matsayi mai kyau a al'umma yana nuna cewa mutum zai yi tunanin kansa. Wannan ra'ayi ya kalubalanci ra'ayin Elizabeth na cewa girma ainihin yaudara ne, duk da yanayin al'umma.
</Callout>

[Hoton wani zane na Renaissance na Charlotte Lucas tana bayyana ra'ayinta game da girman Darcy.] (./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-5/scene-5b.png)

“Wannan gaskiya ce,” in ce Elizabeth.
<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Zan iya gafarta girman da ya yi, idan ba ya sanya ni jin kunya.</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [6]" variant="explanation">
Amincewar Elizabeth na cewa za ta iya gafarta girman Darcy, idan ba ya sanya ta jin kunya, wani muhimmin lokaci ne na tunani game da kansa. Ya nuna cewa bambancinta da shi ya samo asali ne daga abin da ya faru a kansu, maimakon nazarin halayyar sa. Wannan rauni ya sa halayyar ta ta fi fahimta, kuma ya tabbatar da rauninta na farko.
</Callout>

“Girma,” in ce Mary, wacce ta yi alfahari da nazarin halayyar ta, “yana da rauni, ina da imani. Daga duk abin da na karanta, na tabbata cewa yana da rauni; cewa halayyar dan Adam tana da rauni a cikinsa, kuma akwai mutane kaɗan da ba su da rauni game da wani abu ko wani abu, na gaskiya ko na tunanin. Girma da girman kai biyu ne daban, duk da cewa ana amfani da kalmomin biyun a hanya ɗaya. Mutum zai iya yin girma ba tare da yin girman kai ba.”
<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">Girma ya fi dangantaka da ra'ayinmu game da kanmu; girman kai ya fi dangantaka da abin da muke so wasu su yi tunaninmu.</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [7]" variant="explanation">
Nazarin Mary Bennet na girma da girman kai, ya zama babban ra'ayi na littafin. Ta bambanta ra'ayin mutum game da kansa (girma) da abin da mutum yake so wasu su yi tunanin (girman kai). Nazarin ta, duk da cewa ya ɗan yi, yana ba da ingantaccen tsarin don nazarin ayyukan mutane.
</Callout>

"Kare da kare, sannan ku sha kwalba na giya kowace rana.”
