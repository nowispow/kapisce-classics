<Callout title="Analysis [1]" variant="explanation">
Austen ya fara da wani bayani da ya yi kama da gaskiya, amma a asali yana nuna ra'ayin al'umma. Kalmomin 'gaskiya da ake yarda da ita' sun nuna yadda maganar aure take fitowa a matsayin abin da duka ke yarda da shi. Ta hanyar fara da wannan, wannan sashe ya nuna yadda ake neman aure a matsayin al'amari da ya shafi al'umma, ba a matsayin soyayya ne kawai.

</Callout>
Duk da yawan yadda mutum ya san abubuwa da ra'ayinsa, lokacin da ya shiga wani gari, wannan gaskiya ta zama wani abu da aka sanya a cikin tunanin mutanen da ke kusa da shi, har ya zama wani abu da ake ganinsa a matsayin wani abu da ya dace da ɗaya daga cikin 'ya'yansu.

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">
Sashe na biyu ya canza daga bayani zuwa yadda ake lura da al'amura a wani gari. An bayyana wani mutum mai arziƙi a matsayin 'mai mallaka', wanda ya sa mutum ya zama wani abu da ake neman shi. Austen ya yi amfani da wannan yare don nuna ra'ayinsa game da tsarin da 'ya'ya mata ke yin gasa don samun tsaro a cikin arziƙin da maza ke sarrafa.

</Callout>
"Malam Bennet, na gode," matar sa ta ce masa a wani rana, "ka ji cewa an ba Netherfield Park?"

[Hoton Mrs. Bennet yana gaya wa Mr. Bennet cewa an ba Netherfield Park, a cikin ɗaki mai kyau.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-1a.png)

Mr. Bennet ya amsa cewa bai ji ba.

"Amma haka ne," ta amsa; "domin Mrs. Long ta zo nan, kuma ta gaya mani komai."

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">
Maganar farko da Mrs. Bennet ta yi ta nuna cewa akwai bukatar a yi magana da sauri, kuma maganar ta nuna yadda ake yin magana a al'umma, kuma wannan ya zama wani abu da ake amfani da shi don bayyana labarin. Mr. Bennet ya amsa da hankali, kuma wannan ya nuna wani abu mai ban dariya. Maganar da suka yi ta nuna yadda labarin ke nuna bambanci tsakanin damuwa da yadda ake nuna ra'ayi.

</Callout>
Mr. Bennet bai yi wani amsa ba.

"Kada ka so ka san wanda ya ba shi?" matar sa ta ce, da rashin haƙuri.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">Kana so ka gaya mani, kuma ba ni da wani abu da zan ce game da hakan. </Callout>

[Mr. Bennet ya amsa da alƙawari, yayin da Mrs. Bennet ke jira da rashin haƙuri don jin magana.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-2b.png)

Wannan ya isa a matsayin wani abu da za a amsa.

"Yaya, zan gaya maka, Mrs. Long ta ce wani matashi mai arziƙi daga arewacin Ingila ya ba Netherfield; ya zo a ranar Litinin a cikin mota mai doki huɗu don ya ga wurin, kuma ya ji daɗinsa har ya amince da Mr. Morris a nan take; ya ce zai zo ya zauna kafin ranar Michaelmas, kuma wasu ma'aikatansa za su zo wurin kafin ƙarshen mako mai zuwa."

[Mr. Bingley ya zo a cikin mota mai doki huɗu zuwa Netherfield Park yayin da ma'aikata ke shirya don shi ya zauna.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-3b.png)

"Menene sunansa?"

"Bingley."

"Shin yana aure ko ba shi ba?"

"A'a, ba shi ba, na tabbata!"

Wannan sashe yana magana game da kuɗi ba a matsayin ɓangare na labari ba, amma a matsayin abin da ke ƙarfafa duk wani yanke shawara.

[Mrs. Bennet na murnar kuɗin Mr. Bingley yayin da 'ya'yanta ke saurare, suna jin cewa za su iya samun sabbin damar aure.] (./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-4a.png)

"Yaya kuma? Ta yaya zai shafi su?"

"Malam, kaɗai," amsar uwarsa, "yaya za ka kasance mai wahala? Ya kamata ka san cewa ina tunanin auren ɗayansu."

"Shin wannan shi ne nufinsa a wurin?"

"Nufi? Ba haka ba, yaya za ka faɗa haka! Amma akwai yuwuwar za ya ƙaunace ɗayansu, kuma don haka ya kamata ku ziyarce shi da zarar ya iso."

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">

Muhawarar da ake yi game da yin ziyara ta farko tana bayyana ka'idodin da ake bi a al'adar zamantakewar Regency. Mrs. Bennet ta fahimci cewa ba tare da gabatarwa ba, ba za a ba wa iyalai damar yin abubuwa. Austen ya canja al'adar zuwa hanya, inda kira ɗaya ya ƙaddara damar.

</Callout>

[Mrs. Bennet tana jan hankali a kan ziyarar da ake yi wa Mr. Bingley, yayin da Mr. Bennet ya amsa da nuna ɓacin rai.] (./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-5a.png)

"Ba na ganin wani dalili a kan hakan. Ku da 'ya'yan ku ku je - ko kuma ku aika su da kansu, wanda watakila zai zama mafi kyau; domin kamar yadda ku ke da kyau kamar ɗayansu, Mr. Bingley zai fi so ku a cikin wannan taron."

"Malam, kina bayyana ni. Tabbas, na kasance da kyau, amma ba zan iya cewa ina da kyau a yanzu ba."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="definition">

Idan mata na da 'ya'ya mata biyar, ya kamata ta daina tunanin kyawunta. </Callout>

"A irin wannan yanayi, mata ba ta da kyau da za ta tuna."

"Amma, kaɗai, ya kamata ku je ku ga Mr. Bingley lokacin da ya zo wurin."

"Fiye da abin da na yi alkawari, na tabbatar maka."

"Amma ku yi la'akari da 'ya'yanku. Ku yi tunanin yadda hakan zai zama a gare su. Sir William da Lady Lucas sun yanke shawarar zuwa, kawai saboda wannan dalili; domin gaba ɗaya, kamar yadda kuka sani, ba su ziyarci sababbin mutane ba."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="explanation">

Tabbas ya kamata ku je, domin ba zai yiwu a ziyarce shi, idan ku ba ku je ba. </Callout>

"Ku na da yawa, tabbas. Ina iya cewa Mr. Bingley zai yi farin ciki da ganinku; kuma zan aika muku da wasu kalmomi don in tabbatar muku da yardata a auren wanda ya zaɓa daga 'ya'yan mata - kodayake ya kamata in faɗa wani abu game da Lizzy."

"Ina so ku daina yin haka. Lizzy ba ta fi sauran 'ya'yanku ba: kuma ina tabbata ba ta da kyau kamar Jane, kuma ba ta da kyawawan halaye kamar Lydia."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

Dukansu ba su da kyau kuma ba su da ilimi kamar sauran 'ya'yan mata; amma Lizzy na da karin hankali fiye da 'ya'yanta. </Callout>"
“Dukansu ba su da hankali kuma ba su da ilimi kamar sauran 'yan mata; amma Lizzy tana da karfi fiye da 'ya'yanta.”

“Mr. Bennet, ta yaya za ka yi wa 'ya'yanka wani abu mara kyau? Kana jin dadi idan ka bani damuwa. Ba ka da tausayi a kan halina.”

“‘Halina’ na Mr. Bennet, hira ce mai ban dariya, amma kuma tana nuna rashin daidaituwa a rayuwarsu. Ya yi iya kokarinsa don guje wa wani abu, yayin da Mrs. Bennet ta kara ta’addanci. Don haka, abin da ya faru ya nuna halayyar su.

[Hoton Mrs. Bennet tana bayyana halinta, yayin da Mr. Bennet ya amsa da nishi.]

“Kana fahimtar ni, abokiyata. Ina da mutunci ga halinka. Ku ne abokaina na tsawon shekaru. Na ji kana magana game da ku da kyau a shekaru ashirin da suka wuce.”

“Ah, ba ka san abin da nake fuskantar ba.”

“Amma ina fata za ki warke, kuma za ki rayu don ganin wasu matasan da ke samun dubu hudu a shekara sun zo wannan gari.”

“Ba za ta yi amfani da mu ba, idan wasu ashirin suka zo, saboda ba za ki ziyarce su ba.”

“Ki amince da wannan, abokiyata, idan akwai ashirin, zan ziyarce su.”

Mr. Bennet mutum ne mai halaye daban-daban, mai daure kai, mai jin dadi, da kuma mai sauyin ra'ayi, don haka shekaru ashirin da uku ba su isa don matarsa ta fahimci halayyarsa. Tunani na ita ba shi da wahala. Ita mace ce mai ra'ayi, kuma ba ta da ilimi, kuma ba ta da kwanciyar hankali. Idan ta fuskanci wani abu, tana tunanin tana da matsalar halin da take ciki.

Kasuwancin rayuwarta shi ne ta aure 'ya'yanta: kuma ta sami nisha ta hanyar ziyara da labarai.

Wannan bayanin na karshe yana nuna cewar, auren ba shi da daidaituwa. Dalilin Mrs. Bennet da kuma abin da ke ba ta nisha, ya nuna cewa rayuwarta ta keɓance ta hanyar matsi da kuma rashin ikon yin wani abu. Wannan karshen ya canja tattaunawar gida zuwa wani abu mai muhimmanci game da tsammanin jinsi.
