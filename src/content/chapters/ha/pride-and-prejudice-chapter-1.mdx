<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

Wannan gaskiya ce da kowa ya yarda da ita, mutum ɗaya mai arziƙi, yana buƙatar mata.

</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [1]" variant="explanation">

Austen ya fara da wata faɗa da ke nuna cewa yana magana a kan abin da ya fi dacewa, amma a asali yana yin tsokanin al'umma. Kalmar 'gaskiya da kowa ya yarda da ita' tana nuna yadda ana magana game da auren, wanda ake ganin yana da mahimmanci. Ta hanyar fara da wannan, yana nuna yadda ake yin wani abu na al'umma maimakon abin da ke faruwa a tsakanin mutane.

</Callout>

Koda ba a san yadda wannan mutumin yake ji ko yana tunani ba, a lokacin da ya fara zuwa wani gari, wannan gaskiya ta zama wani abu da dukkanin iyalan ke yarda da shi, kuma ana ganin shi a matsayin wanda ya cancanci auren ɗayan 'ya'yan su.

<Callout title="Analysis [2]" variant="explanation">

Sashi na biyu ya sauya daga bayani zuwa yadda ake kula da mutane a wani gari. An bayyana mutum mai arziƙi a matsayin 'mai dukiyar da ya cancanta,' wanda ya sa mutum ya zama abin daraja. Austen ya yi amfani da wannan magana don yin tsokaci kan tsarin inda 'ya'yan mata ke yin gasa don samun tsaro a cikin dukiyar da maza ke sarrafa.

</Callout>

"Kai, Mr. Bennet," in ji matarsa a wani rana, "shin ka ji cewa Netherfield Park an ba shi wani?"

[Mrs. Bennet tana gaya wa Mr. Bennet cewa Netherfield Park an ba shi wani, a cikin daki mai haske.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-1a.png)

Mr. Bennet ya amsa cewa ba ya sani.

"Amma haka ne," in ji matarsa; "domin Mrs. Long ta zo nan, kuma ta gaya mani komai."

<Callout title="Analysis [3]" variant="explanation">

Fadinsa na farko na Mrs. Bennet ya nuna cewa akwai buƙatar magana, kuma yana nuna yadda ake magana a kan abubuwa a al'umma, wanda ya sa har zuwa ƙarshen labarin. Mr. Bennet ya amsa da hankali, kuma hakan ya haifar da abin da ke ban dariya. Maganinsu yana nuna yadda ake magana a kan labarin, wanda ke nuna cewa akwai matsaloli da kuma yadda ake nuna abin da ke faruwa.

</Callout>

Mr. Bennet bai amsa komai ba.

"Shin ba ka so ka san wanda ya ɗauki shi ba?" in ji matarsa, da sauri.

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

Kana so ka gaya mani, kuma ba na da wani abu da zan ce ba.

</Callout>

[Mr. Bennet ya amsa da magana mai ban dariya, yayin da Mrs. Bennet ke jira da sauri don jin abin da ake magana a kai.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-2b.png)
Wannan ya isa a matsayin wata shawara.

"Kai, matata, dole ka sani, Mrs. Long ta ce wani matashi mai arziƙi daga arewacin Ingila ne ya ɗauki Netherfield; ya zo a ranar Litinin cikin keke mai doki huɗu don ganin wajen, kuma ya ji daɗin wajen, har ya yarda da Mr. Morris da sauri; za a ba shi wajen kafin ranar Michaelmas, kuma wasu ma'aikatansa za su zo gidan kafin ƙarshen mako mai zuwa."

[Mr. Bingley ya zo cikin keke mai doki huɗu zuwa Netherfield Park, yayin da ma'aikata ke shiryawa don zama a can.](./metadata/pride-and-prejudice/chapter-1/scene-3b.png)

"Menene sunansa?"

"Bingley."

"Shin yana auren ko kuma bai yi aure ba?"

"A'a, bai yi aure ba, matata, tabbas!"

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="tip">

Wani matashi mai arziƙi; yana da kuɗi sama da ɗari biyar a shekara. Abin da ya fi dacewa ga 'ya'yan mu!

</Callout>

<Callout title="Analysis [4]" variant="explanation">

"Labarin da aka bayar game da yawan kuɗin Bingley ya sa ya zama mutum mai daraja a tsarin aure. Maganar da Mrs. Bennet ta yi game da 'ya'yanta' na nuna cewa soyayya ba ta da muhimmanci kamar kuɗi. Sashen na magana ne game da kuɗi, ba a matsayin wani ɓangare na labari ba, amma a matsayin abin da ke haifar da duk wani shawarar da ake yi.

</Callout>

[Hoto: Mrs. Bennet tana farin ciki game da yawan kuɗin Mr. Bingley yayin da 'ya'yanta suke sauraro, suna tunanin za a yi aure.]

"Yaya? Ta yaya zai shafi su?"

"Malam Bennet, na gode," amsar matarsa, "yaya za ka iya maganar haka? Dole ka san cewa ina tunanin ya auri ɗayansu."

"Shin wannan shi ne abin da yake so?"

"Abin da yake so? Ban gane ba, yaya za ka iya maganar haka! Amma yana da yuwuwa ya ƙaunace ɗayansu, don haka dole ne ku je ku ziyarce shi da zarar ya zo."

<Callout title="Analysis [5]" variant="explanation">

Tattaunawar da ake yi game da yin ziyara ta farko ya nuna ƙa'idodin da ake bi a lokacin. Mrs. Bennet ta fahimci cewa, ba tare da gabatarwa ba, ba za a iya samun damar ziyarce su. Austen ta canza ƙa'idodin zuwa hanyoyin labari, inda kira ɗaya ya bayyana yadda za a samu damar ziyara.

</Callout>

[Hoto: Mrs. Bennet na son a ziyarci Mr. Bingley, yayin da Mr. Bennet ya amsa da ƙarƙasawa.]

"Ba na ganin wani dalili na hakan. Ku da 'ya'yanku ku je – ko kuma ku aika su da kansu, wanda zai fi dacewa; saboda kamar yadda ku ke da kyau kamar su, Mr. Bingley zai fi son ku a wurin."

"Malam Bennet, na gode. Tabbas, ni ma ina da kyau, amma ban da ya kai wani abu ba a yanzu."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="definition">

Idan mace ta haifi 'ya'ya mata biyar, ya kamata ta daina tunanin kyawunta.

</Callout>

"A irin waɗannan yanayi, mace ba ta da kyau sosai da za ta tuna."

"Amma, Malam Bennet, dole ne ku je ku ziyarci Mr. Bingley idan ya zo wannan gari."

"Fiye da abin da na yi alkawari, na tabbata."

"Amma ku yi tunanin 'ya'yanku. Ku yi tunanin yadda zai kasance idan ya auri ɗayansu. Sir William da Lady Lucas sun yanke shawarar zuwa, saboda wannan dalili; saboda a gaskiya, ku sani, ba su ziyarci sababbin mutane ba."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="explanation">

Tabbas, dole ne ku je, saboda ba za mu iya ziyarce shi ba idan ba ku je ba.

</Callout>

"Ku yi hankali, tabbas. Na tabbata Mr. Bingley zai yi farin ciki idan ya gan ku; kuma zan aika muku da wasu kalmomi domin in tabbatar muku cewa ina amince da duk wanda ya zaɓa daga 'ya'yanku – amma dole ne in faɗa abin kyawun Lizzy."

"Ina so ku daina maganar hakan. Lizzy ba ta da kyau fiye da sauran 'ya'yanku: kuma na tabbata ba ta da kyau kamar Jane, kuma ba ta da kyau kamar Lydia."

<Callout title="Memorable Quote" variant="note">

Dukansu suna da banbanci kuma ba su da ilimi kamar sauran 'ya'yanku; amma Lizzy tana da ƙarin basira fiye da 'yan uwanta.

</Callout>

“Dukansu ba su da hankali kuma ba su da ilimi kamar sauran mata; amma Lizzy tana da ƙarin hikima fiye da ’yan uwanta.”

“Mr. Bennet, ta yaya za ka yi wa ’ya’yanka illa ta wannan hanya? Kana jin daɗin damƙani. Ba ka da tausayi a kan halina.”

‘Baƙin ciki’ na Mr. Bennet yana nuna nishaɗi, amma kuma yana bayyana rashin daidaituwar hali a cikin auren. Ya yi amfani da hikima don guje wa magana kai tsaye, yayin da Mrs. Bennet ta kara ta hanyar nuna baƙin ciki. Saboda haka, nishaɗin yana nuna halin ɗayan.

Mr. Bennet yana da halaye daban-daban, yana da hikima, nishaɗi, da kuma rashin tabbas, wanda ya sa ƙwarewar shekaru uku da ashirin bai isa ba don matarsa ta fahimci halinsa. Tunansa bai yi wahala ba. Ita mace ce mai ra’ayi kaɗan, ba ta da ilimi, kuma halinta ba a iya tantancewa. Idan ta ji baƙin ciki, sai ta yi tunanin cewa tana da matsalar jiki.

Ayyukan rayuwarta sun kasance don auren ’ya’yanta: kuma ta samu nishaɗi a ziyarar da kuma labaran da take samu.

Ƙarshen labarin yana nuna cewa auren ba zai yi nasara ba, saboda halayen daban-daban. Dalilin Mrs. Bennet da kuma nishaɗinta sun nuna cewa rayuwarta ta yi iyaka saboda halin da yake a cikin al'umma. Ƙarshen ya canza maganar yau da kullum zuwa wani bita na tsarin da aka sa mata.
